### Design Highlights for a Trustworthy AI Writing Assistant

The page balances a friendly, modern look with clear information hierarchy, making it easy for visitors to understand the product and take action.

- **Color and mood:** A soft indigo brand color paired with white and light gray backgrounds creates a calm, professional feel. The gradient newsletter section adds a subtle energy boost without overwhelming the page.
- **Typography and spacing:** Large, tight headlines draw the eye, while generous section padding and a max-width container keep content readable and focused. The use of system fonts ensures fast loading and a native feel.
- **Card-based layout:** Feature cards, pricing plans, and testimonials all use rounded corners and subtle borders, giving the page a cohesive, approachable structure. Hover effects on cards add a touch of interactivity.
- **Visual storytelling:** The hero image is a custom SVG illustration that mimics an editor interface, reinforcing the product's purpose. The before/after comparison uses color-coded panels to make the value proposition instantly clear.
- **Responsive behavior:** The grid layouts collapse gracefully on smaller screens, and the sticky navigation stays accessible without taking up too much space.
