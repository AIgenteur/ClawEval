Here's a complete, single-file landing page for QuillAI. I went with a warm editorial direction — ink on paper, a vermilion "editor's red pen" accent, serif display type — because a writing assistant should look like it loves language, not like every other SaaS template. The two signature pieces are a live self-typing "AI suggestion" document in the hero and a draggable before/after rewrite comparison.
