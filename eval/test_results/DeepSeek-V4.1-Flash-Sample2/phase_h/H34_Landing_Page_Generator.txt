### Design Highlights for a Trust-Building SaaS Landing Page

The page leans on a calm, confident visual language to make AI writing feel approachable rather than technical.

- **Soft gradients and rounded cards** create a friendly, modern feel that lowers the barrier for first-time visitors.
- **A restrained purple accent** is used consistently for primary actions, icons, and highlights, guiding the eye without overwhelming the content.
- **Generous whitespace and clear section rhythm** let each block breathe, making the page easy to scan and digest.
- **The before/after comparison** uses warm red and green panels to visually reinforce the product's value in a single glance.
- **Subtle hover lifts and smooth scrolling** add a layer of polish that makes the page feel responsive and alive.
